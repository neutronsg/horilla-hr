# HR quick guide: monthly payroll

## 1. Employment dates and working days

Open the employee's active **Payroll → Contract**. Enter the actual start and end dates and the full monthly **Basic Salary**.

Set **Payroll Workweek** to **Monday–Friday**, **Monday–Saturday**, or **Every day** as appropriate. Choose **Use shift / company weekly off days** for a custom schedule, and configure the shift's days and minimum hours. Days of 5 hours or less count as half a day for incomplete-month salary. Do not reduce the contractual monthly salary manually for a new joiner.

Horilla clips payroll to employment dates and calculates:

**Monthly salary ÷ full month's scheduled working days × payable working days**.

Public holidays remain included on scheduled working days. Rest/non-working days are excluded. Approved unpaid leave reduces pay once. Example: S$8,400 monthly salary, Monday–Friday, joining 7 September 2026 → **18/22 × S$8,400 = S$6,872.73** before deductions.

## 2. Salary structure and deductions

An assigned **Salary Structure** defines the recurring deductions HR wants to apply. An empty deduction list means **no recurring deductions**, even if old recurring components remain directly assigned to the employee. Individually recorded advances, installments and dated one-off deductions are handled separately. The structure detail shows these additional employee assignments and whether they are excluded.

When no salary structure is assigned, the existing employee-specific / company deduction rules apply. For a structure that includes CPF or a self-help group contribution, HR must select the appropriate **Statutory Deduction Type** on that component.

HR controls **CPF Exempt** and **SDL Exempt** on the contract. They default to off; the system does not infer exemptions from employee type, department or institution. When HR enables an exemption, record its basis and supporting document reference. CPF exemption excludes CPF and CPF-linked CDAC/ECF/SINDA contributions. MBMF has separate rules. Employer contributions and SDL do not reduce employee net pay.

## 3. Review a draft

Check **Contract Basic Pay** (monthly contractual amount), **Earned Basic Pay** (prorated amount), and the displayed working-day ratio. Confirm the separate advance entries and that **gross pay − total deductions = net pay**. Exemption settings are saved with generated payslips so a later contract edit does not rewrite historical employer SDL.

Only correct draft payslips automatically. Confirmed or paid payslips require HR's normal correction process. For annual and sick leave setup, see [the leave quick guide](hr-mom-leave-quick-setup.md).
